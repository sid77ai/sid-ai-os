# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Siddharth — the operator of this system. Single expert user who built it, knows every agent by name, runs it daily as a personal AI operating system. Eventually may be a product for other power users. Design for one expert operator now; keep patterns clean enough to scale later.

## Product Purpose

SID AI-OS is a personal multi-agent operations center built on Hermes Agent. It turns a generic agent runner into a mission control surface: task routing, structured debates, activity logs, inbox triage, skills inventory, audit trails, and morning briefings — all unified under one cockpit. Success looks like the operator glancing at the dashboard and immediately knowing the state of their entire AI operation.

## Brand Personality

Command · Warm · Precise

Operational confidence without cold-industrial sterility. A cockpit that feels lived-in and personal. The operator trusts the system; the system trusts the operator back.

## Anti-references

- **Cyberpunk / neon AI aesthetic**: Dark mode with glowing rings, terminal-green matrices, floating HUD elements. Every AI product reaches for this. SID AI-OS is the opposite: warm, cream, grounded.
- **Generic SaaS dashboard**: Notion-clone beige, Airtable-style card grids, left-nav + content-area layout with a "welcome" header. Forgettable and interchangeable.

## Design Principles

1. **Signal over clutter** — every element must be earning its place by conveying state, information, or action. Decoration that doesn't work is waste.
2. **Warm command** — the tone is a war room run by a person who cares, not a cold monitoring console. The cream palette and display typography carry this; never let the UI feel clinical.
3. **Trust the operator** — no onboarding copy, no "are you sure?" modals for routine actions, no empty states that explain the obvious. The operator built this system. Respect that.
4. **Dense but legible** — mission control surfaces are information-dense by necessity. Pack it in; just make the hierarchy do its job.
5. **Consistent vocabulary, memorable moments** — standard affordances everywhere, personality in the moments that matter (a council verdict, an alert firing, an approval clearing).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single known user for now. WCAG AA contrast ratios maintained. Keyboard navigation is secondary but worth implementing as the product scales. No known reduced-motion requirements.
